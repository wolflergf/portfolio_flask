Ever found your AI agents stuck in an endless loop? 🤯 Or wondered where your API costs went after a complex conversation?

I'm thrilled to introduce **LoopHalter** (aka AgentTracker), now officially on PyPI! ✨

For anyone building sophisticated multi-agent systems with frameworks like CrewAI, AutoGen, or LangChain, you know the challenges: opaque conversations, unpredictable costs, and those frustrating infinite loops.

LoopHalter is designed to bring order to the chaos by offering:

*   💬 **Comprehensive Message Tracking:** Get a full, queryable history of every interaction, sender, recipient, and token used.
*   💰 **Granular Cost Calculation:** Understand and predict your API expenses across models like GPT-4, GPT-3.5-turbo.

Imagine debugging with a clear, timestamped log, or preventing budget overruns *before* they happen. LoopHalter makes your AI agent development more reliable, cost-efficient, and transparent.

Easy to integrate (`pip install LoopHalter`) and ready to fit seamlessly into your existing projects.
