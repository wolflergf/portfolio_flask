I'm excited to share Argutum, an AI assistant I've developed that brings a truly novel twist to our daily interactions with artificial intelligence. For many of us in development and data science, AI tools like ChatGPT or Claude are indispensable for brainstorming, coding, debugging, and problem-solving. My vision for Argutum was to build a fully capable AI, but with a fundamental difference: I designed it to pay *you* for the quality of your input messages.

What does this mean for your daily work? You use Argutum exactly as you would any other AI assistant – ask your complex coding questions, explore data analysis ideas, or work through intricate technical problems. However, every single message you send is scored in real-time across five key dimensions: complexity, novelty, structure, domain expertise, and depth. These criteria are particularly relevant for developers and data scientists, whose prompts often naturally embody these qualities, moving beyond simple queries to nuanced, expert-level interactions.

The practical benefits are straightforward: I've created an opportunity to monetize an activity you're likely already doing. If your message achieves a score of 75 or higher, you can earn up to $0.085 per message, with lower tiers also offering compensation. This not only offers a potential new income stream without a subscription fee, but it also provides immediate, valuable feedback on your prompt engineering skills. Seeing how your prompts are graded can help you refine your ability to craft clearer, more effective, and more insightful queries – a valuable skill in itself – and get paid for doing so. Payouts are conveniently handled via PayPal once your balance hits just $0.50.

Ultimately, my aim with Argutum is to transform routine AI interaction into a genuinely rewarding experience. For developers and data scientists who consistently craft detailed, intelligent, and domain-expert prompts, Argutum offers a unique platform to have their contributions recognized and financially compensated. It's an incentive to push the boundaries of your AI interactions, knowing that your valuable input helps train better models, and you get paid for your expertise.
